## Notes on what was rejected

Roughly 270 of the file's findings did not qualify and were not carried forward. The recurring disqualifiers:

- **Needs a new AWS API call or a new client**: resolving EFS access points to file systems, `DescribeTargetHealth` for registration, `DescribeContainerInstances` for ECS hosts, `GetCrawlers` for the S3↔Glue join, us-east-1-pinned ACM/CloudWatch/Lambda clients for CloudFront, per-bucket S3 region routing.
- **Needs a shared-helper or contract refactor**: the many "same fact computed two ways" findings (backup coverage, ARN→row-ID normalisation, alarm dimension matching, launch-template resolution) whose stated fix is "one predicate both sides call".
- **Schema / identity changes**: cluster-qualified `ecs-svc` IDs, ARN-keyed `elb` rows, composite `codeartifact` IDs, namespaced inline-policy IDs.
- **Product decisions**: whether a pivot should exist at all, whether a proven zero should become Unknown, how a finding should be worded when the spec and the code disagree.
- **Pagination loops**: judged case by case — a `NextToken` loop plus a truncation flag is more than a handful of lines in most of these call sites, so they were left out unless the fix was purely `relatedResultTrunc(..., next != nil)`.
- Tests, fixtures and snapshot-collector-only findings were excluded per the brief.
