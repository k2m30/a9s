# v3.57.1 — the error log counts what it names

A patch on v3.57.0 from the live read-only walk that gated it.

## Fixed

The `!` error log's partial-failure line reads "failed for N of M IDs".
For a check that makes several calls per resource, N counted failed
calls while M counted resources, so a denied role produced lines such
as "bucket posture failed for 220 of 50 IDs" and "failed for 2 of 1
IDs". Both numbers now count resources: N is how many had at least one
refused call, and it can never exceed M. The per-cause groups after the
colon are unchanged.

The ECR posture line named `DescribeImages` as the operation while the
refused calls were the lifecycle-policy and repository-policy reads. It
now names the check, the way the other multi-call posture lines do.

## CI

The nightly race matrix gives Windows the time it needs under the race
detector: it finishes in about 18 minutes there against 8 on macOS and
Ubuntu, and the previous 20-minute ceiling killed it whenever the shuffle
ran long.
